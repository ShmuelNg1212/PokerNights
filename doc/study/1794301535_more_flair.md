# Study: more flair

Date: 2026-11-10. Mode: design. Stage: study. The human asked: "explore things i can do to add more flair to this webapp".

## What already has flair

The app has a lot of motion. Do not add more of the same kind.

- Front door chip, arrival after login, card-to-set carry.
- Set start: "Shuffle up and deal" overture and the felt light.
- Seat draw played back on every phone.
- Pot digit roll, press springs, sheets, toasts, checklist ticks.
- Count-up ticks, the balanced double rule, "Settled" moment.
- Recap reveal with result bars, dealt share card.

## Limits on new flair

These come from PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md and AGENTS.md.

1. No casino imagery and no raster decoration.
2. Money does not count through other values. Success shows only after the server accepts.
3. Reduced motion removes movement. Without JavaScript the page still works.
4. One signature moment per event, within 900 ms. The overture is a recorded exception.
5. Losses are never called out.
6. An ambient, looping felt animation conflicts with the motion system (found 2026-11-06).
7. No front-end framework, no WebSocket, no new server.

## Gaps

- The night has a start (the overture) but no end. Closing a session goes straight to the recap.
- Rebuys and cash-outs change figures, but nothing shows money move between a player and the pot.
- Nothing connects one night to the next: no streaks, no records, no season.
- A player has no identity of their own. The chip colour is given, not chosen.
- Every phone feels the same. There is no haptic or sound, even as an option.

## Ideas

Size: S fits one commit. M is one plan of 3 to 5 tasks. L needs a study of its own.

### During the night

| # | Idea | What the person sees | Fit and risk | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chip to the pot** | When a rebuy is accepted, one chip in the player's colour flies from their row token into the pot. On a cash-out, it flies from the pot to the row. Every phone sees it at the next poll. | Shows money move without counting it. Must start only from a server-accepted morph. Must not fire on a full page replacement. | M |
| 2 | **Last orbit call** | The host taps "Last orbit". Every phone shows a Brass banner, "Last orbit · 10 min", with a quiet countdown. | Functional flair. New write: needs `request_id`, audit and a version bump. Timer belongs to the set (rule 10). | M |
| 3 | **Optional haptics** | On Android, a short tick on an accepted action and on the overture's landing. Off by default; a switch in Profile. | iPhone Safari has no vibration API. Overture text says "no vibration"; the human must change that. | S |
| 4 | **Optional sound** | A soft chip clack on accepted rebuy, a shuffle on the overture. Off by default. | Dim room, late night: risky. Needs small audio files; check size and licence. | M |

### End of the night

| # | Idea | What the person sees | Fit and risk | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **"That's the night" finale** | A bookend to the overture when the host closes a session. Each player's chip slides into a ranked stack. The top result gets a Brass edge. "That's the night" stamps in. Then the recap rises. | Mirrors the overture, so the night has a shape. Under 900 ms or a second recorded exception. Do not rank losses visibly. | M |
| 6 | **Night titles** | The recap gets a short generated title from frozen facts: "The longest night", "Six-handed, one big pot", "Everyone broke even". | Words only. Must be true and never mock a loser. Rules live in the settlement layer and need tests first. | S–M |
| 7 | **Share card styles** | Two or three layouts for the share picture: the current one, a "winner's card" for the top result, a compact one for a busy chat. | Reuses the canvas drawing. No new font or colour. | S–M |

### Between nights

| # | Idea | What the person sees | Fit and risk | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Streaks and records** | On a player's page and in the recap's Highlights: "3 winning sessions in a row", "Personal best", "10th session", "First win". | All from closed sessions, as Stats already does. State only good news or neutral counts. | M |
| 9 | **Season review** | Once a month or year, a "Your October" page and share card: sessions played, best night, most played with, longest night, record. Like a year-in-review. | The biggest flair per effort. Read-only, nothing new stored. Pesos and chips stay separate. | L |
| 10 | **Group milestones** | The group page marks "50th session" or "1 year of poker nights" with a one-time banner. | Small, warm. Once per person per milestone. | S |

## Recommendation

Take these three first. Each fills a gap, not a moment that already moves.

1. **#5 "That's the night" finale.** It closes the shape the overture opened. It reuses the overture's stage code.
2. **#1 Chip to the pot.** It is the most visible live moment. It runs many times a night, so keep it short (about 400 ms).
3. **#8 Streaks and records**, then **#9 Season review** on top of it. #8 supplies the facts that #9 shows.

Leave #12 and #4 until a real night shows a need. Both work against a recorded design rule or the room.
