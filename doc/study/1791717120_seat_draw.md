# Seat draw before a set starts: study

Date: 2026-10-11, Asia/Manila.

## Request

The human said: "I want to add a seat draw before a set starts. this can be added as an optional step after opening the session to players, before starting the set. I want it to be a cool animation displaying a sample poker table and the winner of the draw (dealer seat) can choose their seat on the table. When a draw is started cook up a cool animation to draw who gets the dealer seat and the other players get random positions also meaning how far they sit clockwise from the dealer is randomized (basic poker positions). When the winner of the draw chooses a seat, play cool animations to placing the players on their respective seats based on the winner's seat of choice. Make sure to utilize motion and UI skills, mcp, and plugins for this feature."

Asked four questions, the human chose:

- **Who chooses the seat:** the winner on their own phone, or the host for them.
- **Where it plays:** on every phone on the set page.
- **How many seats:** one per player. Six players is a six-seat table.
- **After the start:** the seating stays on the set page. A late player has no seat until the host gives them one.

## What exists today

- No seating. A table has a seat count (2 to 12) that only limits how many can join. A player at the table is a `Participant` with a `join_order`. PRODUCT.md names seating as a future feature; the wiki says "no seating".
- A set goes Draft → Open → In play. An open set's page shows "Get this set ready" with four steps, and the host's one main button is "Start the set".
- The set page asks the server every 4 seconds for a new snapshot when the set's `version` changed, and replaces its live region. So a draw written by a service reaches every phone within about 4 seconds with no new channel.
- Motion 14 with named presets; a drawn "felt" panel, the rail colour and the players' chip tokens with initials already exist.

## Findings

1. **The server must draw.** One result for every phone. `secrets.SystemRandom` shuffles the players at the table; the first is the dealer, the rest are 1, 2, 3… seats clockwise from the dealer. Each phone only plays back a result that already exists, so two phones can never show different winners.
2. **Positions follow from the distance.** Clockwise from the dealer: small blind, big blind, under the gun, then middle positions, hijack, cutoff. With two players the dealer is the small blind. With three there is no position after the big blind. The names come from the number of players; they are computed, not stored.
3. **Positions are true for the first hand only.** The dealer button moves each hand and the app does not track hands. During play the table shows who sits where and who dealt first, not "SB" and "BB".
4. **What the winner's choice changes.** With one seat per player, the order around the table is fixed by the draw. The chosen seat turns the picture: where the dealer sits on the drawn table, with everyone else following clockwise. At the real table the winner sits where they like and the rest take their order from the screen.
5. **It is not money, but it is a record people may argue about.** A draw is kept; drawing again keeps the earlier draw as replaced, with who asked for it. Nothing is edited or deleted.
6. **The players can change after a draw.** Before the start, a player joining or being taken off makes the draw wrong. The draw is then marked out of date and the host is asked to draw again. After the start, a late player is placed by the host between two seated players; a player who leaves keeps their place, shown as away.
7. **A second phone learns late.** With a 4-second poll, the winner's tap would reach the others up to 4 seconds later. While a draw waits for a seat the page can ask every 1.5 seconds, for at most two minutes.
8. **Each phone must play the draw once.** As the closing recap does: remembered per draw in the browser. A phone that arrives after the draw sees the finished table, not a replay, unless it asks.
9. **The live region is replaced on every update.** The stage where the draw plays must live outside it, fed by the snapshot, or an update would cut the animation.
10. **Two rules of DESIGN.md are touched.**
    - "A signature moment within 900ms, once." A draw with suspense needs about 4 seconds, and the seating about 2. It can be skipped by a tap and never blocks a control.
    - "Don't add casino imagery." The table is drawn in the app's own shapes and colours (the rail, the felt, the chip tokens, a dealer disc), with no cards, no photo and no raster file.
11. **Without JavaScript, and under reduced motion,** the result is a list in seat order and a still table.
12. **The user tests on the live build.** A switch, and a failure that leaves the set page as it is today.

## Options for where it plays

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| A. A full-screen stage that opens over the set page | Room for a table on a phone; one focused moment; closes back to the page | A modal that opens by itself on a player's phone |
| B. Inside the "Get this set ready" panel | No overlay | About 220px tall on a phone: too small for twelve seats and a moving draw |
| C. A separate page | Simplest | A player must navigate there; "every phone" would need a redirect |

Recommend A, opening by itself once per draw, with one Close.

## Risks

| Risk | Effect | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Phones show the draw at different seconds | Someone hears the winner before seeing it | Unavoidable with polling; the faster poll keeps it to about a second and a half |
| The winner has no login or no phone | Nobody can choose | The host can choose for them |
| The winner never chooses | The set would wait for them | The draw never blocks Start; an unchosen draw seats the dealer at the top seat when play starts |
| Twelve players on a 320px phone | Names overlap | Tokens with initials on the table; full names in the list under it |
| The stage covers a host's work | Annoyance | It opens only for a new draw, once; Close and Escape; the page behind keeps updating |
