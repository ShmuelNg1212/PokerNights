# Study: whole-app UI critique (2026-11-19)

## Scope and method

- Target: every screen in `templates/`, seen as host `hana`, player `ben` and viewer `mara`.
- Data: a fresh SQLite database seeded with `seed.py`, `seed_end_set.py`, `seed_night.py`, `seed_opening.py` and `seed_home.py`.
- Widths: 390 px for every screen, 1280 px for the main screens, and 320 px for one set in play.
- Method: impeccable critique, dual agent. Assessment A was a design review. Assessment B ran the detector and made in-browser measurements. Neither saw the other's output.
- References: Splitwise, Settle Up, Apple Wallet and Apple Cash, Monzo, Revolut, Linear, Things 3, Apple Stocks and sports scoreboards.
- No project file changed. No money was written.

## Strengths

1. The set in play is the reference screen. It has the felt, the large tabular pot, timer and blinds tiles, one card per player and one bone primary action.
2. Money language and error recovery: signed and arrowed results, hatched losses (not colour alone), "₱100 missing", and "The app records payments; it does not move money."
3. The app takes each viewer's own view. The player's own row comes first, and the home card says "Miguel owes you ₱400".

## Priority issues

### 1. [P1] A player's chip identity is not stable

- The same person has a different colour and different initials on different screens. Examples: Ben is pink "BN" on set 3, blue "BE" on Stats, and orange "B" on session 1. Miguel is red on set 3 and blue on sessions 12 and 19.
- Cause: `web/templatetags/table_tags.py:37` gives the colour as `(join_order - 1) % 10 + 1` in each set. `member_token` (line 49) uses `member.pk`. Initials depend on the other names on the page (lines 22–36).
- DESIGN.md calls c1–c10 "the ten recurring chip identities". The chip is how a host finds a player at a glance.
- Suggestion: give each group member one colour and one two-letter label, and use them on every screen. This changes a rule in DESIGN.md, so it needs approval.

### 2. [P1] The open-set and draft dock overloads the host before Start

- The open set shows about ten controls. The dock has "Host controls ⌄" above "More host controls ⌄" (`templates/web/_host_controls.html:4–5` and `:69`).
- Suggestion: remove the "Host controls" heading row on phones. Move "Join this set" into the players list as a "+ You" row, or into More host controls. Leave "Start the set" with its checkbox alone in the dock.

### 3. [P2] The recap that opens by itself covers the money

- On a session page, the recap sheet opens over "You pay ₱850". The × shows a focus ring on load. In one capture, ben's page was not scrolled to the top after close.
- Suggestion: do not open the sheet automatically. Show a "Recap ready" card under the lead, as Apple Wallet and Splitwise put the amount first. If the sheet stays automatic, do not show the focus ring on load, and keep the page at the top after close. Check the scroll on a phone.

### 4. [P2] The count-up dock figure has no label, and its message repeats

- The dock shows "₱3,550 of ₱8,500 bought in" with no label. The host has to remember that ₱3,550 is counted plus cashed out.
- The same sentence appears in the books, the books summary and the dock (`ledger/queries.py:394`).
- Suggestion: label the figure "Counted + cashed out". Keep the sentence in the dock only.

### 5. [P2] A mismatch looks like an emergency, and the override sits beside the fix

- The panel is red, the dock line is red and the primary button has a red outline. The difference can be one miscounted chip.
- "Finalize with an override" sits right under the error.
- Suggestion: make the panel neutral and colour only the amount with Down. In this state, put the player counts before the books. Move the override into More host controls.

### 6. [P2] Cash out and Reverse share one cramped sheet

- Reverse is a short thumb move from Cash out.
- The "Tap a player to cash out" hint also shows at 1280 px, where every row already has a Cash out button (`templates/web/_players.html:11`).
- Suggestion: put corrections under a hairline with a heading. Ask for a confirmation before a reversal. Hide the hint from 900 px.

### 7. [P3] Words and formats are inconsistent

- Quick amount "Default" against "Usual buy-in" (`templates/web/_buy_in_form.html:11`).
- "23:07" against "11:07 PM".
- Set settings says "Configure rake before the first buy-in" on a set whose rake is locked (`templates/games/settings_form.html:9`).
- The New session date uses the browser format "11/19/2026".

### 8. [P3] The home group card shows noise to players

- The gear shows to players as well as hosts (`templates/web/_group_card.html:6`).
- The token stack shows the first six members alphabetically, so it shows nobody in particular.
- "And 3 more unpaid transfers in past sessions." is not a link.

## Detector evidence (Assessment B)

- CLI: 101 findings. 99 `design-system-color` findings are false positives: the tool could not resolve `{% static 'css/app.css' %}` and read the browser's default black. The broken-image finding at `_share.html:8` is a false positive: `share.js:279` sets the src. One finding is real: `templates/offline.html:14` uses 1.75rem, which is off the type ramp.
- In the browser, all low-contrast findings on chips are false positives. The detector read the edge gradient. On the solid face, c1–c10 measure 5.71:1 to 10.26:1. The active pill at 1.0:1 is also a false positive: `.pill-marker` paints its fill.
- Real signal: much text under 14 px. Examples: /g/1/ has 76 elements (58 at 12 px), / has 37 and /s/2/ has 28. Nothing is under 11 px. On a phone read in a dim room, 12 px secondary text is small.
- Pattern flags left for design judgement: radial halo and stripes (they belong to the felt and the buy-in edges, so they stay), nested cards on home and count-up, and one straggler word on count-up.
- No horizontal overflow at 390 or 320 px. Headings are in order. The only target under 44 px is the off-screen skip link.

## Patterns from professional apps worth adopting

- **Monzo / Revolut**: after a money write, show a short confirmation that names the amount and the person ("Rebuy ₱1,000 · Miguel"), with Undo for a few seconds. This would catch the most common slip before Reverse with a reason is needed. It must stay append-only: Undo records a reversal.
- **Splitwise / Settle Up**: "I paid" from the payer's side, as a pending mark that the payee confirms, plus "Remind" through share.
- **Linear**: one primary action per view, with secondary actions in one menu. This applies to the open-set dock.
- **Things 3**: a small moment of completion when the last transfer is marked paid ("Everyone is square").
- **Apple Stocks / scoreboards**: one stable colour per participant on every view (issue 1).
- **Apple Wallet**: the amount first, then status, then history. The recap belongs in history, not in a modal.

## Minor observations

- The rebuy amount field shows "1000" without grouping. The row shows "₱1,000".
- Settings fields for blinds and buy-ins show no ₱ prefix.
- A player who left shows "₱1,000" large and "Cashed out ₱2,650" small. Their net matters more.
- "8 · 2 seats free" on the set and "7 at the table" on home count different things.
- On Stats, the sort chips scroll sideways with a cut-off "R…" and no scroll cue.
- Stats says "You have no closed session yet." to a host who hosted five sessions.
- In the count-up progress bar, the segment types differ by colour alone.

## Questions for the human

1. Should a chip belong to the person (in the group) instead of to the set?
2. Does every row need Rebuy, or can a quick "same again" gesture remove most of those buttons?
3. Should a mismatch at 2 a.m. look like an alarm or like a calm search for ₱100?
4. Is the session page for paying people or for remembering the night?

### Detector and browser (Assessment B)

- CLI: 103 findings. 101 `design-system-color` and 1 `broken-image` are false positives: the scan could not read `app.css` through `{% static %}`, and `share.js` fills the preview image. 1 real: `templates/offline.html:14` uses 1.75rem, which is `--h-page` but not on DESIGN.md's frontmatter ramp (known, kept: the page cannot load `app.css`).
