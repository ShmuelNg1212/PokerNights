# Table alerts in the header: plan

Status: approved 2026-10-16 with the five decisions as written. Date: 2026-10-16, Asia/Manila. Study: [Table alerts in the header](../study/1792163902_alerts_bell.md).

## Outcome

The table alerts control is a bell in the header of every signed-in page, no longer a panel at the foot of Your groups. A person whose alerts are off sees the bell's panel open by itself once per app session, on Your groups, and can turn alerts on with one tap.

Mode: Operate. Visual authority: the built Rack in DESIGN.md. Shaped with the design (impeccable) and motion (animate) skills. No migration, no dependency, no new switch.

## Behaviour

| Where | What the person sees |
|---|---|
| Header, push possible, alerts off | An outline bell before the username. Tap: the panel with "Get a notification when a host adds you to a set." and **Turn on** |
| Header, alerts on | A filled bell. Tap: "On for this phone." with **Turn off** |
| Header, blocked | A slashed bell in Bone Dim. Tap: "Alerts are blocked for PokerNights in this phone's settings." |
| Header, iPhone in Safari | An outline bell. Tap: "On an iPhone, alerts work from the Home Screen app." and **Add to Home Screen**, which opens the existing home-screen sheet |
| Header, push impossible and installing would not help | No bell |
| Reminder | The first time Your groups shows in a window, for a person with a group whose permission is unasked: the panel opens by itself in the ask state, with **Not now** beside Turn on. It waits for the arrival (1100ms, or 600ms without it) and gives way to the home-screen sheet or any open dialog, trying again on the next visit to Your groups. Never on another page. Once shown, not again in that window |
| Foot of Your groups | The panel is gone. "Add to home screen" / "Join with a link" stay |
| `PUSH_ALERTS=False` or no VAPID keys | No bell, no panel, no reminder |

The panel is not modal. Focus goes to its first action. Escape, a tap outside it, or the bell closes it, and focus returns to the bell. The bell's name gives the state ("Table alerts, on"), with `aria-expanded`.

## Look

- **Bell:** 48px target, 22px glyph in the icon stroke. Outline for off and install, filled for on, slashed in Bone Dim for blocked. No dot or badge.
- **Panel:** under the bell, right-aligned to the header, `min(300px, 100vw − 32px)`, Rail 2, 1px Line edge, surface radius, sheet shadow, a 10px caret at the bell. Title 1rem/750, sentence in Bone Dim, actions 48px. Neutral **Turn on**, quiet **Not now** (both as today).
- **Header below 360px:** the username is hidden, so the brand, bell and Log out keep one line.

## Motion

- **Open:** opacity with `fade` (180ms). Scale 0.96 → 1 and 4px drop with `shift`, from the caret (top right).
- **Close:** `leave` (200ms), opacity and 4px up.
- **Reminder only:** the bell swings once around its top, −14°, 10°, −6°, 0°, over 600ms with an ease-out. This is the one new keyframe set.
- **Turned on:** the outline bell cross-fades to the filled one, which rises from 0.9 with `arrive`. The lines cross-fade and the tick springs, as today.
- **Failure:** the message is nudged (`nudge`), as today.
- **Reduced motion:** every state appears at once; no swing.
- Only `transform` and `opacity` animate. The first keyframe is set before Motion starts.

## Decisions for the human

The three decisions in the study's Request section were settled in questions on 2026-10-16. Approving the plan also accepts these:

1. The reminder is the bell's own panel opening by itself, not a separate bubble or toast.
2. The 30-day "Not now" is replaced by once per window. The old `pn-alerts-later` key is removed.
3. No switch to bring back the old foot panel. `PUSH_ALERTS=False` remains the way to turn the whole feature off.
4. Below 360px the username in the header is hidden.
5. A blocked phone gets the slashed bell and no reminder.

## Rendezvous (2026-10-16)

Built on `feat/alerts-bell` with the design (impeccable) and motion (animate) skills. Released 2026-10-16 (see Release).

- **As planned:** the bell in the header of every signed-in page, with outline, filled and slashed glyphs and the state in its name. The anchored panel with the ask, on, blocked and install states. The reminder is the panel opening by itself once per window on Your groups, with Not now and one swing of the bell. The 30-day key is removed. The name is hidden below 360px. There is no new switch, migration or dependency. `push_alerts` and the public key moved to `config.pwa.flags`.
- **Changed while building:**
  1. **The panel hangs from the header's right edge, not from the bell.** Anchored to the bell, it ran 26px off the left of a 390px screen, because Log out sits to the bell's right. Its caret is placed under the bell by the script (`--alerts-caret`).
  2. **The panel is on Rail, not Rail 2.** On Rail 2, Turn on and the quiet Not now looked the same.
  3. **The glyph change is a CSS transition**, not Motion's `arrive`. It can be interrupted, and it needs no script. The swing is a CSS keyframe that runs only without reduced motion.
  4. **A refresh of Your groups no longer waits for the panel.** The first build counted the open panel as busy, as an open sheet is. An ignored reminder then stopped the page from refreshing; `arrivals.mjs` caught it. Now the panel stays open through a refresh, because the bell is permanent and a same-page visit leaves it alone. It closes when the person goes to another screen (`turbo:visit` to another path).
  5. **The reminder waits 1.4 seconds after a login (0.8 otherwise)**, not 1.1 and 0.6. That way the home-screen sheet, which opens at 1.1 seconds, takes its turn first, and the reminder gives way to it.
  6. **The reminder does not move focus.** It is not something the person opened, so the polite announcer reads its sentence instead. A tap on the bell does move focus to the first action.
  7. `icon` now adds a per-icon class (`icon-bell` and so on). One door test that matches `main`'s exact attributes now includes `data-alerts-remind`.
- **Verified:** 1,216 tests on SQLite (15 skipped, PostgreSQL only) and on PostgreSQL. `alerts_bell.mjs` passes 45 of 45 and `arrivals.mjs` 45 of 45, each on a fresh seed. The 11 panel checks moved out of `arrivals.mjs`. Screenshots were checked at 320, 390 and 1280px.
