# Claim links: plan

Status: approved 2026-10-09 ("approved."); decisions as recommended except 8. Date: 2026-10-09, Asia/Manila. Study: [Claim links](../study/1791527468_claim_links.md).

## Outcome

A regular who has always been a name on the roster gets their own login without losing their history. The host creates a claim link for that player and sends it. The player opens it, signs up or logs in, and is that player from then on: same results, same Stats, and their own rebuys and counts from their phone.

Mode: Operate. Visual authority: the built Rack in DESIGN.md, its Entry pages addendum and its Roster addendum. No new colour, font or asset. One migration (`groups.0005`, a new table; nothing existing changes).

## Behaviour

### 1. The host creates the link

Group settings → Players → "Manage {name}" gains **Create claim link** for a player without a login. It is not offered for a member who has a login.

After the tap, the Players section shows once:

> **Claim link for Tito Boy.** Send it to Tito Boy only. Whoever opens it becomes Tito Boy in this group. It works once and expires Oct 16, 21:40.

with the link in a read-only field and the Copy button, as a reset link is shown. The link is never shown again; only its hash is stored.

While a link is live, the player's "Manage" block says "Claim link active until Oct 16, 21:40" with **Cancel link**. Creating another link cancels the earlier one.

### 2. The player opens the link

Opening the link changes nothing. What happens next depends on who opens it:

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Signed out | Sign up, naming the group, with "Already have an account? Log in". A new account becomes the player at once and lands in the group. |
| Logged in, not in the group | The confirm page (below). |
| Logged in, already in the group with nothing recorded | The confirm page, with one more line saying their empty entry is replaced. |
| Logged in, already in the group with games recorded | A refusal: "You already have games recorded in this group as {their name}. Two records cannot be joined yet. Ask a host of the group." |
| Logged in, removed from the group earlier with games recorded | A refusal: "You were in this group as {their name}. Ask a host to bring you back." |

The confirm page uses the entry frame:

- Heading **Join {group} as {name}**
- "A host kept {name}'s games here before you had a login. Claiming makes them yours: you will see {name}'s results, and you can record your own rebuys and counts."
- "You are logged in as **{username}**." with a quiet **Log out** for someone on the wrong account.
- Where it applies: "You are already in this group as {their name}, with nothing recorded. That entry is replaced by {name}."
- Button **Claim {name}** ("Claiming…" while it is sent) and a quiet **Not now**.

After the claim the person lands on the group with "Welcome to {group}. You're in as {name}." The player keeps the roster name, and can change it under "Change your name". The row in Players loses its No login badge.

A link that is unknown, cancelled, used or expired, or whose player was removed or already has a login, shows **This claim link doesn't work** with one reason and "Ask a host of the group for a new one". Status 404.

### 3. What a claim changes and what it does not

- The member row is the same one. No result, transfer, payment, seat or count is moved or rewritten.
- If the account had an empty entry in the group, that entry is deleted. If that entry was a host, the claimed player becomes a host.
- A claim cannot be undone in the app. A wrong claim is handled by removing that player and adding the right person; the wiki says so.

### 4. Switch

`CLAIM_LINKS=False` in Vercel turns the feature off without a release: the button is hidden, creating is refused, and every claim link says it is not valid. Default on.

## Decisions for the human

Approving accepts the recommendations unless you say otherwise. The reasons are in the study.

1. **Whoever opens the link first becomes that player.** The host chooses who gets it; it works once, lasts 7 days and can be cancelled. Recommended: accept, as with invites.
2. **An account that already joined by invite and has not played can still claim.** Its empty entry is deleted and replaced. Recommended, because this is the common case.
3. **An account that already has games recorded in the group is refused.** Joining two records is a change to money records and needs its own cycle.
4. **A claim cannot be undone in the app.** The remedy is to remove the player and add the right person.
5. **A newcomer who signs up from the link becomes the player at once,** with no confirm page. An existing account gets the confirm page.
6. **The link lasts 7 days.** The alternative is 24 hours, as a reset link.
7. **The player keeps the roster name,** not the username.
8. **Order of release.** This builds on the roster screens, which are on `feat/roster-management` and not released. Recommended: release roster management first, check it on your phone, then build this on `main`. The alternative is to build this on top of the roster branch and release both together.
9. **The wording** in sections 1 and 2. Change any of it.

## Implementation

Tests are written first for each step. `groups` imports neither `games`, `ledger` nor `settlement`.

1. **Model (`groups/models.py`, migration `0005`).** `ClaimLink`: `member`, `token_hash` (unique, SHA-256), `expires_at`, `used_at`, `used_by`, `revoked_at`, `created_by`, `created_at`. Registered read-only in the admin.
2. **`groups/services.py` (the only writer).**
   - `create_claim_link(actor, member_id) -> (member, link, token)`: host only, group lock, the member is active and has no login, cancel the member's live links, create the new one with a 32-byte random token, `audit.record("claim_link.issued")`. The token is returned once.
   - `cancel_claim_link(actor, member_id)`: `audit.record("claim_link.cancelled")` when there was one.
   - `live_claim_links(member_ids) -> dict`: read-only, one query.
   - `usable_claim_link(token, *, lock=False)`: the link, or a `RuleError` with the reason.
   - `claim_preview(user, token)`: the link, the player, the account's own row in the group if any, and whether that row has records. Read-only. "Has records" is asked of the database with Django's deletion collector, which reports a `PROTECT` refusal without deleting; `groups` learns nothing about the apps above it.
   - `claim_member(user, token) -> Member`: lock the link row, the group, then the member row; check everything again; delete the account's empty row first so the one-row-per-account rule is free, then set `member.user`, carry a host role over, stamp `used_at` and `used_by`, `audit.record("member.claimed")` and, where a row was replaced, `"member.replaced"`. The same account repeating a claim it already made gets the member back and nothing is written.
3. **Sign-up hooks (`groups/access.py`, `groups/apps.py`).** `claim_vouches`, `claim_group_name` and `claim_after_signup`, registered beside the invite hooks, so a claim link opens sign-up on the invite-only address, names the group on the entry pages, and finishes the claim for a new account.
4. **Views and addresses (`groups`).**
   - `POST g/<group>/members/<member>/claim-link/` and `…/claim-link/cancel/`, resolved through `member_for`. The new address is kept in the login session for one display, as a reset link is.
   - `claim/<token>/`: exempt from the login requirement, `never_cache`, `Referrer-Policy: same-origin` (the reset cycle found `no-referrer` breaks the form). GET shows the refusal (404), sends a signed-out visitor to Sign up, or shows the confirm page. POST claims and opens the group.
5. **Templates.** `groups/claim.html` in the entry frame (confirm, refusal, dead link); `web/_group_settings.html` (the action, the live-link line, the once-only notice with the Copy field).
6. **`web/views.py`.** The settings tab reads the live claim links for the roster in one query and takes the once-only notice from the session.
7. **Setting.** `CLAIM_LINKS = env.bool("CLAIM_LINKS", default=True)`.
8. **Design.** The claim page and the host's block are built with the design and motion skills, as the roster screens were, inside DESIGN.md's tokens and motion presets. The once-only notice and the changed row use the existing accepted-change mark.
9. **Tests.**
   - `groups/tests/test_claim_links.py`: issue stores a hash and not the token; a second issue cancels the first; only a host of the group can issue or cancel (403, and 404 across groups); refused for a member with a login, a removed member and an archived group; each dead-link reason; a GET never uses the link; a claim by an account outside the group keeps the member row and its results; a claim by an account with an empty row deletes that row and keeps a host role; refusals for an account with records, active and removed; a repeat by the same account; a second account after a claim is refused; both audit events carry the group; the switch off.
   - With play: `settlement/tests`: after a claim, Stats and the roster's sessions line show the same figures for the same member; the claimed player's own rebuy is accepted in a running set; an account whose own row has a seat, a result or a transfer is refused in each case.
   - Sign-up: a signed-out visitor from a claim link may sign up where sign-up needs an invite, the entry pages name the group, and the new account is the player on arrival; a link that died in the meantime leaves the new account outside the group with the reason shown.
   - `web/tests`: the action appears only for hosts and only on players without a login; the notice shows once; the live-link line; the settings tab's query count does not grow with the roster.
   - PostgreSQL only: two accounts using one link at once (exactly one becomes the player); a claim and a removal of the same player at once; two issues at once (one live link remains).
   - Browser check `claim.mjs` with a seed, at 320, 390 and 1280px: the host creates and copies the link; a signed-out browser signs up and lands in the group as the player with the old sessions; a second seed case where an account with an empty entry confirms and replaces it; an account with records is refused; the used link is refused; 48px targets, contrast, focus, no horizontal overflow, and every step working without JavaScript.
10. **Verify.** SQLite and PostgreSQL suites. `claim.mjs`, then `entry.mjs`, `reset.mjs`, `copy.mjs` and `roster.mjs`, which share the pages. Captures inspected.
11. **Sync docs.** Wiki features, architecture (the table, the lock order, the collector check) and deployment (the switch, "Who can get in"), PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md addenda, roadmap Stage 5, TODO. SPEC.md is the human's.

Work is done on branch `feat/claim-links` with Conventional Commits. Nothing is pushed without an instruction.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1. A host creates a claim link for a player without a login, and a newcomer who opens it signs up and is that player, with the player's past results and Stats unchanged and now theirs.
- AC2. An existing account outside the group claims after one confirm page. An account with an empty entry in the group claims, and that entry is gone.
- AC3. An account with games recorded in the group is refused, and nothing changes.
- AC4. A link works once, stops after 7 days, and stops when cancelled or replaced. Opening it without confirming never uses it.
- AC5. Only a host of the player's group can create or cancel a link, and none can be created for a member with a login.
- AC6. The database holds no usable token: only hashes.
- AC7. Two accounts using one link at the same moment produce exactly one claim (PostgreSQL).
- AC8. No result, transfer, payment, seat or count row is changed by a claim.
- AC9. After a claim the player records their own rebuy in a running set.
- AC10. Issuing, cancelling and using a link each leave an audit event naming who and when.
- AC11. `CLAIM_LINKS=False` removes the feature without a release.
- AC12. One additive migration. Existing tests pass on SQLite and PostgreSQL.

## Out of scope

- Joining two member rows that both have records.
- Undoing a claim.
- A claim link for a member who already has a login, or for a removed player.
- Sending the link by email, SMS or any channel the app would send on.
- A "change my password" screen.

## Rollback

Set `CLAIM_LINKS=False` in Vercel for an immediate stop. To remove the code, revert the feature commits; the new table can stay unused, so no migration needs reversing. Claims already made stay: those players keep their logins, and an empty entry that was replaced is not restored.
