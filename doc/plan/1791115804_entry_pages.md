# Entry pages revamp: plan

Status: approved 2026-10-04 ("approved. i accept your recommendations."). Date: 2026-10-04, Asia/Manila. Study: [Entry pages revamp](../study/1791115770_entry_pages.md).

## Outcome

The four pages a person passes through to get into the app look like the front door of PokerNights and tell a newcomer what they are joining: Log in, Sign up, "Sign-up needs an invite" and "Join group".

Mode: Operate. Visual authority: the built Rack in DESIGN.md. No new colour, font or raster asset.

## Design brief

**Job and audience.** A host returning to the app, or a player who was sent an invite link in a group chat and has never seen the app. On a phone, usually. They want to get in with as little reading as possible.

**Thesis.** One centred column that says who we are, then gets out of the way. The same composition as the existing first-group screen, so the entry pages and the first screen after them read as one sequence.

**The entry frame (all four pages), top to bottom.**

1. The chip-and-crescent mark at 72 px, centred.
2. The name "PokerNights" in the heavy condensed style the header brand uses, larger.
3. One line about the app (Log in and "needs an invite" only): **"Keeps the books for your home poker game: buy-ins, cash-outs and who pays whom."**
4. The task heading: "Log in", "Create your account", "Join {group}", "Sign-up needs an invite".
5. The form, with full-width 48 px controls and the primary action as a full-width bone button.
6. One quiet line with the alternate path ("No account? Sign up").

The site header is left out on these pages while signed out, because the lockup in the page replaces it. On "Join group" the person is signed in, so the header with Log out stays and the lockup is the small mark only.

The column is 440 px wide and centred at every width. On desktop it sits in the upper part of the page; there is no second column and no panel around the form.

**Invite context.** When Log in or Sign up is reached from a usable invite link, a brass-ruled line above the heading reads **"You're invited to {group}"**, and Sign up's heading becomes "Create your account to join {group}". A used-up, expired or revoked invite shows nothing.

**Forms.**

| Page | Fields and help |
|---|---|
| Log in | Username. Password with a Show button. Button: Log in. |
| Sign up | Username, help: "The name you log in with. Letters, digits and @ . + - _". Password with a Show button, help: "At least 8 characters. Not a common password and not only digits." Repeat password, no help. Button: Create account. |

- The second password field stays, because the app has no password reset yet.
- **Show button.** A text button inside the field's right edge: "Show" / "Hide", 48 px target, `aria-pressed`. One button on Sign up controls both password fields. It appears only when JavaScript runs. Field names and `autocomplete` values do not change, so password managers keep working.
- **Errors.** A refused login shows one notice above the fields with Django's existing message. A refused sign-up shows each error under its field with the negative border, including the specific password rule that was broken. The username stays; passwords come back empty.

**States and ranges.** Empty; filled by a password manager; wrong login; each sign-up error (taken username, short password, common password, mismatch); arriving with and without an invite; an invite that stopped being valid; group names up to 60 characters and unbroken; 320, 390 and 1280 px; no JavaScript; reduced motion; keyboard only.

**Motion.** None added.

## Decisions for the human

Approving the plan accepts these unless you say otherwise.

1. **The one line about the app**: "Keeps the books for your home poker game: buy-ins, cash-outs and who pays whom." Change the wording if you prefer another.
2. **Sign up keeps two password fields.** The Show button was offered with the option of asking once. I recommend keeping both until password reset exists.
3. **Invite wording**: "You're invited to {group}". Only the group name is shown to a signed-out person; the "Join group" page (signed in) also shows the number of players.
4. **Desktop** uses the same centred column, not a two-column layout.

## Implementation

1. **Invite context without breaking app direction.** `accounts/signup.py` gains a second registry, `INVITERS`, and `invited_to(request, next_path) -> str | None`. `groups` registers a function beside `invite_vouches` that returns the group name for a usable invite path. Read-only.
2. **Views (`accounts`).** `signup` passes `invited_to`. Log in becomes a small subclass of Django's `LoginView` that adds `invited_to` to the context. No change to authentication, `safe_next` or the sign-up gate.
3. **Form (`accounts/forms.py`).** `SignupForm` sets the two help texts, the label "Repeat password" and no help on the second field. Validators and field names are unchanged.
4. **Templates.**
   - `base.html`: a `{% block header %}` around the site header so a page can leave it out.
   - `partials/entry_head.html`: mark, name, optional line, optional invite line.
   - `registration/login.html`, `accounts/signup.html`, `accounts/signup_closed.html`, `groups/invite_accept.html`: the entry frame. `form.form-section` and the field names stay, so existing tests and scripts keep working.
   - `groups/invite_accept.html` shows the player count from the view.
5. **CSS (`app.css`).** One `.entry-page` block: the centred 440 px column, lockup, invite line, full-width button, the password field with its Show button. `home-first` shares the lockup rules instead of duplicating them.
6. **JavaScript.** `static/js/password.js` (about 20 lines): finds `[data-password-toggle]`, shows it, switches the fields between `password` and `text`, and switches back to `password` on submit. Loaded only on Log in and Sign up.
7. **Tests first.**
   - `accounts/tests`: the invite line appears for a usable invite on both pages and not for a missing, revoked, expired or used-up one, nor for an off-site `next`; the group name is escaped; the help texts and label; the wrong-login notice; refused sign-up keeps the username and shows the specific password rule; `autocomplete` values; "needs an invite" still returns 403.
   - `groups/tests`: the join page shows the group and its player count; the error page.
   - Browser check `entry.mjs` on a fresh temporary database at 320, 390 and 1280 px: no overflow against the requested width with a 60-character unbroken group name; every control at least 48 px; visible focus order through the form; the Show button toggles both fields, keeps focus and resets on submit; submission and all four pages without JavaScript; contrast of the new text pairs; logging in through the existing selectors.
8. **Verify.** SQLite and PostgreSQL suites. `entry.mjs`, then `dock.mjs` and `archive.mjs`, which log in through these pages. Captures inspected at 320, 390 and 1280 px.
9. **Sync docs.** DESIGN.md (replace the "Account and supporting forms" lines about login and signup with a dated addendum), wiki features, browser README, TODO.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1. Log in, Sign up, "needs an invite" and "Join group" share the centred frame with the mark and name, and the primary action is full width.
- AC2. A person arriving from a usable invite link sees the group name on Log in and Sign up; nobody sees a group name for an invalid link.
- AC3. Sign up fits a 390 × 844 phone without scrolling when empty.
- AC4. The Show button works on both pages, is absent without JavaScript, and does not stop a password manager from filling the form.
- AC5. Authentication, the sign-up gate, the password rules, `next` handling and status codes are unchanged.
- AC6. Existing tests pass on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and the browser scripts still log in.

## Out of scope

- Password reset, email, social login, "remember me".
- A separate landing or marketing page.
- Changing the password rules or the username rules.
- The log-out flow and the 403/404 pages.
- The site header on signed-in pages.

## Rollback

Revert the feature commits. No migration and no data change.

## Progress and blockers

2026-10-04: Study and plan complete after one discovery round (branded entry; the whole entry flow; invite name, shorter rules, Show button). The human approved with “approved. i accept your recommendations.”

2026-10-04 execution on `feat/entry-pages`. Tests were written first.

Changes from the plan:

1. **The invite sentence sits under the heading as plain text**, not above it with a brass rule. A label above a heading and a coloured side rule are both patterns the design floor refuses. Sign up's heading stays “Create your account”; the group is named in the sentence.
2. **Username help is “The name you log in with.”** The list of allowed characters wrapped badly at 320px and Django's error already names them when one is refused.
3. **A broken password rule is reported under Password**, not under Repeat password as Django does by default. Found in the first capture round: the error sat under the wrong field. The rules themselves are unchanged and tested.
4. **The browser scripts' shared login line changed** to `form.form-section [type=submit]`. The Show button is now the first button in the form, so the old selector would have pressed it. The study listed these selectors as something to keep; the form class and field names are kept, the button selector could not be.
5. **`home-first` was left alone.** Sharing its lockup rules would have changed the first-group screen, which is outside this cycle.
6. **Join group has a “Not now” link** to Your groups, so the page has a way out other than the header.
