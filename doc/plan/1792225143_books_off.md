# When the books do not balance: plan

Status: asked for and built 2026-10-17 after a critique of the end-of-set screens (24/40, `.impeccable/critique/`). The four parts were built in parallel by sub-agents on separate worktrees and merged on `feat/books-off`. Not released.

## Outcome

When every player is cashed out and the books differ, the screen helps the host find the error instead of only announcing it: the gap in words, the players' in/out and net on their rows, the row whose hand-typed cash-out differs from its count marked, and the host bar's button taking the host there. An override no longer looks like success. No migration, no dependency, no new colour, font or asset.

## Parts

1. **The panel** (`_books.html`, `_count_total.html`, `Balance.headline/gap_sentence/causes/absorbers/covered_text`, `CountTotal.coverage_text`; `_balance.html` deleted, unused).
2. **The rows** (`_count_rows.html`, `PlayerLine.cash_net`, `count_gaps()`, `books_off` in the view). The suspect rule: a final cash-out with no linked count, and a count voided at or after it with a different amount (an exception cash-out typed by hand voids the count in force). A `CashOut.replaced_count` link would make it exact; not added.
3. **The host bar** (`_host_controls.html`, new `static/js/gap.js`).
4. **Motion** (`static/js/changes.js`, on `live:updating`/`live:updated`).
5. **Integration**: one wording ("too much"/"missing") across panel and bar; the override named and warn everywhere, counts.js included; "still to count" only while someone is; the desktop running total in Down once every player is cashed out; the leave also when an override covers the gap.

## Known limits

- A fix made in two steps (reverse, then re-enter) shows no leave: the books pass through "not counted yet".
- On phones a cashed-out row is about 54px (94px when suspect) while the books are off, since the net takes a second line.

## After the second critique (25/40)

The two P1s: a suspect is marked only when its difference runs the way the books are off (one that explains the whole gap is `full` and is taken first; one that explains part says "₱150 more than counted"), and with no suspect the host bar's button opens "Where to look". The running total says "too much" everywhere and shows the gap alone once every player is cashed out. Left for later (P2/P3): a quieter line for players, the override stated once with its note, the first row under the host bar at 320.
